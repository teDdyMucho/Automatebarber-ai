# US barbershop pricing research

Every price below was read on the shop's own page or booking page on **22 October 2026**.
The system's default prices are the medians of the 15 core independent shops, rounded to the
nearest $5 (12 of the 15 price their cut in $5 steps).

Independent mid-to-upper shops (median cut $50) charge about twice what the value chains do,
in the upper half of Booksy's $30–65 band.

## Rebooking intervals

Cut every 4 weeks ("a regular customer who comes in every four weeks", Eddie's; "monthly
appointments", State Street). Fades every 2–3 weeks
([Pete & Pedro](https://peteandpedro.com/pages/pedros-pointers-how-often-should-men-get-a-haircut),
[Barberstake](https://www.barberstake.com/blog/how-often-should-men-get-a-haircut/)). Beards every
1–2 weeks for crisp lines ([Booksy](https://booksy.com/en-us/s/beard-shaping/45042_33615)).
Line-ups every 2 weeks ([The Spot](https://thespotbarbershop.com/line-up-haircut-guide-to-clean-sharp-edges/)).
The system's intervals (4/3/4/4/2/2/2/1/3 weeks) already sit inside these and were kept.

## Hours (27 shops)

Median weekday 9am–7pm; a third stay open to 8–9pm on some weekdays. Saturday: 23 of 27 open,
median close 6pm. Sunday: 10 of 27 closed. Monday: 6 of 27 closed. Median 62 open hours a
week. **This system's hours (71 a week) are realistic for a busy urban shop but on the long
side; Friday 8am–9pm is the least typical part.** Kept as they were (content unchanged).

## Caveats

- Shops that only book through Squire could not be read (it blocks fetching), so the sample
  leans toward shops with their own site, Square or Setmore. Floyd's 99 shows prices only
  inside its booking widget and was not verified.
- Thin data: Senior and Military (2 shops), Full Service (6), Kids (8).
- Some menus are "from" or tiered prices; the entry tier was used.
